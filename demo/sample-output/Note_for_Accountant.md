## 4. Upcoming: Budget 2027 MSME Tax Rate Reduction (For Planning)

Budget 2027 (announced 9 October 2026) includes a 1 percentage point reduction in SME tax rates, expected to apply from Year of Assessment 2027:

| Band | Current Rate (YA 2026) | New Rate (YA 2027, expected) |
|------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| First RM150,000 | 15% | 14% |
| RM150,001–RM600,000 | 17% | 16% |
| Above RM600,000 | 24% | 24% (unchanged) |

**Estimated saving for Lina's Bakery (based on current PBT of RM93,500):**
- Current: 15% x RM93,500 = RM14,025
- From YA 2027: 14% x RM93,500 = RM13,090
- **Estimated annual saving: ~RM935/year**

If the business grows and chargeable income reaches RM150,000: saving = RM1,500/year.
At maximum (chargeable income of RM600,000+): saving = up to RM6,000/year.

**Status:** Not yet in force. Implementation details pending from LHDN/MOF. The reduced rates are expected to be applied automatically via self-assessment filing for YA 2027 — no separate application anticipated.

**Action:** No action needed now. For planning purposes, please factor in the reduced rates when projecting tax for FY2027.
