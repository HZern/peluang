## 3. ✅ E-Invoicing Special Tax Deduction

**What it is:** You can deduct up to RM50,000 per year from your taxable income for costs related to e-invoicing and other ESG (environmental, social, governance) expenses. This reduces the tax you pay.

**How much you could save:** The e-invoicing module in your KedaiDigital quotation costs RM2,400 for a 12-month subscription. Claiming this as a deduction saves you about RM360 per year in tax (RM2,400 x 15% marginal rate). If you have other ESG expenses (like energy-efficient equipment), you could save up to RM7,500 per year if you claim the full RM50,000.

**Documents ready:**
- KedaiDigital quotation showing the e-invoicing module at RM2,400 (QT-2026-0917)

**Still needed:**
- The actual paid invoice/receipt from KedaiDigital after you implement the e-invoicing module — the quotation alone is not enough for tax filing. You need a tax invoice showing payment.
- If you want to claim other ESG expenses, keep all receipts and invoices for those too.

**Apply here:** No separate application. Your accountant (LK Tan & Associates) claims this deduction in your annual tax return for Year of Assessment 2026. Just make sure to give them the e-invoicing invoice/receipt.

## 4. ✅ Stamp Duty Exemption on Employment Contracts

**What it is:** Since 1 January 2026, employment contracts for staff earning RM3,000/month or below are exempt from stamp duty. This used to be limited to salaries up to RM300/month — the threshold has been raised significantly.

**How much you could save:** About RM20–50. Stamp duty is RM10 per contract. If you stamp contracts for the 2 new staff you plan to hire in November 2026 (both at ~RM1,900/month), you save RM20. If you can also stamp the contracts of your 3 existing qualifying staff (Nurul Izzah at RM2,000, Chong Wei Jie at RM1,900, Aisyah at RM1,800), you could save another RM30 — but check with your accountant first, as those contracts were signed before 2026.

**Documents ready:**
- Payroll Summary August 2026 (showing the 3 qualifying employees and their salaries)
- Plan to hire 2 new staff from November 2026 at ~RM1,900/month each

**Still needed:**
- New employment contracts for the 2 planned hires — must be dated on or after 1 January 2026
- A quick check with LK Tan & Associates or LHDN on whether existing pre-2026 contracts can still benefit

**Apply here:** No application needed. The exemption applies automatically when you submit the contracts for stamping with LHDN. Just make sure the contracts are for salaries at or below RM3,000/month and dated after 1 January 2026.

## 5. 🟡 GGSM4 Financing Guarantee

**What it is:** A government-backed guarantee scheme that covers up to 80% of a bank loan, making it much easier to get financing with less collateral. It's not a grant — it's a guarantee that helps you get a loan. You still borrow and repay the money, but the government guarantee makes the bank more willing to lend.

**How much it helps:** For your planned second deck oven (~RM45,000), the guarantee could cover up to 80% (RM36,000) if the bank needs collateral. You pay a guarantee fee of 0.75% per year (minimum RM500). This could be the difference between getting the loan and not getting it.

**Documents ready:**
- SSM Company Profile
- Management Accounts FY2025
- Bank statement August 2026
- Payroll Summary August 2026

**Still needed:**
- Bank statements for 6–12 months (currently only August 2026 is on file — need to gather more months)
- FY2024 financial statements, if available (the scheme asks for 1–2 years of financials)
- **Two questions for the owner** (see email):
  1. Does the bakery hold a Halal certificate?
  2. Are either of the shareholders Bumiputera?
  
  The GGSM4 scheme focuses on specific sectors: Bumiputera, Halal, Tourism, and High Growth High Value. Food & Beverage is not on this list by itself, but if you have Halal certification or Bumiputera status, you qualify. If neither applies, there are other SJPP schemes (like SJMK) with broader coverage that you could ask your bank about.

**Apply here:** Apply through any participating bank (22 financial institutions participate). Tell the bank officer you want to use the GGSM4 government guarantee scheme. Do not apply to SJPP directly — the bank handles it. Reference: https://rakyathub.my/cara-mohon-sjpp-jaminan-pembiayaan-2026

**Deadline:** Applications open until 31 December 2026 or until the fund is fully used.
