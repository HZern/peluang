# Put your business documents here

Peluang reads whatever you put in this folder. The more of these you add, the fewer questions it will email you:

| Document | Why |
|---|---|
| SSM company / business profile | Company type, registration, owners and ownership %, start date |
| Latest audited or management accounts (or last 2 months of bank statements) | Sales, profit, paid-up capital |
| Payroll summary | Staff count, salaries, join dates |
| Quotations from suppliers (e.g. a POS, website or e-invoicing system) | Grant amounts |
| A short note about your business and plans (or an email to us) | Plans like hiring or buying equipment |

**Do not** put IC/MyKad copies here. You add those yourself when you submit an application.

Then type in WorkBuddy: `Check what government help my business is missing out on.`
